Booking & pickup

Use the pickup request form or call our booking desk. Share the pickup address, delivery address, shipment type, weight and preferred service. We will confirm pickup and hand over the AWB number.

Rates & claims

Pricing, insurance & claims

Cost depends on chargeable weight (actual or volumetric, whichever is higher), distance, service level and optional insurance. Use our volumetric calculator or request a quote.

Transit insurance is optional and must be added at booking. Without it, no claim can be raised for loss or damage during transit. See insurance & claims for details.

Notify us within 7 days of delivery with your AWB number, photos of the damage and a description of the contents. Our team will guide you through the insurer's claim process.
