Transit insurance & claims

Protect the declared value of your shipment against loss or damage in transit. Add insurance at booking and our team will guide you through every step if something goes wrong.

Important

  • Insurance must be added before dispatch
  • Declared value must match the contents
  • Claims need proof of value and damage

What is covered

  • Loss of the shipment during transit
  • Physical damage caused during handling or transit
  • Coverage up to the declared value, subject to policy terms
  • International and domestic shipments, when declared at booking

What is not covered

  • Prohibited or undeclared items
  • Inherent vice, wear and tear or poor packaging
  • Delays caused by customs, weather or force majeure
  • Loss or damage where no insurance was purchased
No insurance, no claim: if a shipment is not enrolled in transit insurance at the time of booking, no claim can be raised for loss or damage during transit.
Claims process

How to raise a claim

Follow these steps so we can process your claim quickly.

01

Notify us

Contact our team within 7 days of delivery or expected delivery with your AWB number.

02

Share evidence

Provide photos of the packaging and damage, plus the invoice or proof of value.

03

Assessment

We review the documents with the insurer and may request additional information.

04

Resolution

Approved claims are settled as per the policy terms, with status updates from our team.
